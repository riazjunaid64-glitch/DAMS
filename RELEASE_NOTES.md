## Finance settings on the shared library (KAN-96)

Finance > Settings is rebuilt on the shared components for desktop and phone, with the same rules and data. One header, **Finance settings**, whose button follows the tab: **Add category** on both category tabs, **Add vendor** on Vendors, none on Financial year (**Add** on a phone). Four tabs: **Expense categories** and **Revenue categories** with their counts (retired ones included), **Vendors** and **Financial year**; on a phone they are one **Section** dropdown (the shared tabs take the dropdown's word; every other screen keeps "Show"). The page opens on Expense categories and keeps the chosen tab after a save.

While the tax rates are unchecked, Expense categories (only) shows one gold notice, "Tax rates have not been checked by an accountant yet.", with **Mark as checked**. Both category tabs have a search (name, description, and the tax section for expenses), a **Status** filter that opens on Active (Retired, All), Reset, and pages of 20 cut in the browser (numbers on a desktop, Load more on a phone). Expense categories show the tax section ("No tax" when nothing is withheld), the filer and non-filer rates, the **Yearly limit** ("From Rs 1" for a limit of 0) and how many payments use it; revenue categories show the description and usage, and no longer the order. A retired category carries a grey **Retired** badge. Each row has a pencil and a ⋯ menu with **Retire** (used and active) or **Delete** (never used); a retired category in use has no ⋯ (Edit switches it back on). Both ask in the shared confirm ("Retire Cement? It is used by 1 payment, so it can't be deleted. …" / "Delete Steel / iron / rebar? It has never been used, so it will be removed for good."), and the toast says what the server actually did.

Add / Edit category has no Code or Display order field: a new category sends an empty code (the server makes it from the name) and order 900, an edit sends both back as saved. **Deduct withholding tax** off hides the rates, the tax section and the yearly limit and sends the three numbers as 0 (the section is kept, as an expense records it either way); a category with no tax that is switched on starts with empty rates. A used category's popup says "Used by 12 payments. They keep the rate they were entered at." The revenue popup keeps **Order in lists**. The vendor popup offers Filer, Non-filer and Unknown (taxed as non-filer), **Checked on the FBR taxpayer list today** with "Last checked …" under it, and Active on Edit only. Every refusal shows inside its popup and every save shows a toast.

Vendors pages on the server, 20 at a time, with a **Filer status** filter beside the search; each row shows the phone (or "—"), the filer badge, NTN or CNIC (or "Not recorded"), paid this year, tax withheld and payments, and an **Inactive** badge. **Financial year** has two cards: the KAN-91 year and go-live card, and **Tax rates check** ("Checked by accountant · Aug 11, 2026 by …" with **Clear check**, or "Not checked yet" with **Mark as checked**). Saving the year now says "Finance settings saved." Mark as checked and Clear check always send the saved month, never one picked but not saved, and no go-live date; a month or go-live date typed but not saved stays in the card through them.

Shared pieces: `StatusBadge` knows **Retired**, **Checked by accountant** and **Not checked yet**; one `OptionalLabel` replaces the "(optional)" helper each finance and commission popup had its own copy of (four of them showed it in capitals). The Tax to FBR list footer, its page cutting and the no-wrap phone figure are now shared finance pieces used by both pages. `RevenueCategoriesPanel.tsx` is deleted.

Server: `GET /api/finance/vendors` takes `filerStatus` (Filer, NonFiler, Unknown) and answers `totalCount` on every page. "An expense category with this code already exists." now reads "Another expense category already has a name that looks the same. Change the name.", the revenue one "A revenue category with this name, or one that looks the same, already exists.", and the two "add it under Finance ▸ Settings ▸ Expense heads & rates" messages point at "Finance settings > Expense categories". No migration.

The "WHT payable" tab leaves Finance settings and becomes its own page, **Finance > Tax to FBR** (`/finance/tax`), for Admin and Accountant, on desktop and phone. The top of the page is one sum with the server's figures: **Brought forward + Withheld from suppliers − Deposited to FBR = Still owed to FBR**. The four cards are all-time and do not change with From / To; Brought forward is always shown, also when it is Rs 0, and Still owed is the gold card. Under them, with a date range set, one line reads "In the selected period: withheld Rs 412,300 from 18 payments to 6 suppliers · deposited Rs 412,000"; its supplier count is the number of rows in the By supplier list, so the line always matches the list. The "by section" chips, the long explanation and the "Withheld all time" box are gone.

Two tabs share the From / To filter (both dates or neither) and Export: **Deposits to FBR** (date, challan / CPR, paid from, period covered, amount; pencil to edit, ⋯ to delete) and **Withheld by supplier** (the s.165 statement: supplier, NTN / CNIC, filer status, section, paid in total, tax withheld, paid to supplier). The status is the one the tax was withheld under on the day, so one supplier can have two rows. The server sends both lists whole, so the page cuts them into pages of 20 itself (numbers on a desktop, **Load more** on a phone); a delete that empties the last page steps back one page. The supplier list loads when its tab opens, or at once when a date range is set because the line above counts its rows.

Record deposit and Edit deposit are shared popups (full screen on a phone). A new deposit opens on what is owed, with "Owed now: Rs 184,350" under the amount; Save stays off until the account, a positive amount and the date are there, the date cannot be in the future and the period end cannot be before its start. One idempotency key is made per open popup, so a retry after a lost answer does not pay the same challan twice, and every server rule (more than was owed on that date, a repeated challan number, a changed record) shows inside the popup with what was typed. Delete asks "Delete deposit CPR-0923-118? Rs 412,000 goes back to being owed to FBR." in the shared confirm and shows a refusal in a toast.

The Finance home notice's **View** button and **Tax to FBR** link, and the **Record deposit** button on the Tax payable account, open the new page. Finance settings has no WHT payable tab any more.

Shared pieces: `StatusBadge` knows **Filer** (green), **Non-filer** (red) and **Unknown** (orange), and a highlighted `StatCard` shows its number in gold as well as its label. The WHT statement, deposits and payable-summary calls take an optional abort signal.

Server: the supplier statement (and its CSV export) now lists every payee typed as free text on its own line. It used to fold all of them, per section and filer status, into one line named after whichever name sorted first, so a statement for ten unlinked suppliers showed one. A linked vendor is still one line however its name was spelled on older rows, and the totals are unchanged.

## Manage accounts and the go-live date (KAN-91)

Manage accounts is rebuilt on the shared components for desktop and phone: one header, seven groups (Cash & bank, Cash held by staff, Fixed assets, Work in progress, Receivables, Liabilities, Capital) each with a subtotal row, no stat boxes and no "All accounts total", filters for search, Type, Holder and Status, and a pencil and a ⋯ menu on each row (an active system account has the pencil only). Clicking a row opens Account details with the figures, the tax held for FBR, and the transactions with a running balance. Add and Edit have no Display order (Edit sends the saved one back). Deactivate and Reactivate ask in a popup, and a refusal shows as a toast instead of a browser alert. A red notice shows the difference when the opening debits and credits do not match.

**The opening balance is typed once, in Add / Edit account**, on the account's own side (a minus sign means the other side), at any time, also after go-live. Changing it, or typing a non-zero one on a new account once a go-live date is saved, asks "Change the opening balance?" first, and every change writes a history row (who, from, to), including a new account created with a non-zero opening (0 → figure). Customer deposits, Customer receivables and Commission payable take no typed opening: the server refuses a new figure ("... is worked out from bookings and commissions ...") and accepts a legacy one saved unchanged or cleared to 0 (the form offers "Clear opening balance" for such an account). Loan accounts still cannot open below zero. A non-zero opening now counts as history, so such an account cannot change type or be deleted.

**The go-live date is a plain setting** in Finance settings > Financial year (shared date picker, not after today). Everything that read the date of the committed opening balance set now reads it through `FinanceDateRules.BaselineAsync`, so no report or posting-date check changes. A saved date cannot be emptied; moving it asks first, and the server refuses a later date while any record is dated before it (it names the records and the earliest day). The posting message is now "... cannot be before the go-live date (dd MMM yyyy). Everything up to that date is already inside the opening balances." The **Opening balances tab is gone** from Finance settings, and so are its panel, the `OpeningBalanceService`, the `api/finance/opening-balances` endpoints (`GET current`, `POST`, `PUT {id}`, `POST {id}/commit`, `POST {id}/reopen`), their DTOs and entities. The partner statement's go-live row now says "Opening balance".

API: `FinanceSettingsDto` and `SaveFinanceSettingsDto` carry `goLiveDate` (null on save = unchanged). `FinanceAccountsOverviewDto` carries `openingDebitTotal`, `openingCreditTotal`, `goLiveDate` and `holderNames` (every account's holder, distinct and sorted). `FinanceAccount` and `FinanceSetting` are now in the correction trail (`FinanceRecordAudits`), which records the opening balance and the go-live date; `UpdatedAt` is left out, so saving identical values writes nothing.

Deploying this runs the `MoveGoLiveDateToFinanceSettings` migration, **one-way**. It adds `FinanceSettings.GoLiveDate` and fills it with the date of the committed opening balance set (none when no set was committed, so there are still no date limits). It copies the old audit lines (`OpeningBalanceSet`) and every per-account note or amount (`FinanceAccount`, action `OpeningNote`) into `FinanceRecordAudits`, then drops `OpeningBalanceAuditEntries`, `OpeningBalanceEntries` and `OpeningBalanceSets`. `Down` recreates the three tables empty. A set that was reopened keeps its date, and amounts edited in it but never recommitted are not applied (they survive only as history rows); a draft that was never committed leaves the go-live date empty and its amounts survive only as history rows.

**Before deploying:** back up, then run `SELECT [Id],[AsAtDate],[IsCommitted],[CommittedAt],(SELECT COUNT(*) FROM [OpeningBalanceEntries] e WHERE e.[OpeningBalanceSetId]=s.[Id]) AS Entries FROM [OpeningBalanceSets] s;` and, for a reopened set, `SELECT a.[Name], a.[OpeningBalance], e.[DebitAmount], e.[CreditAmount] FROM [OpeningBalanceEntries] e JOIN [FinanceAccounts] a ON a.[Id]=e.[FinanceAccountId];` to see whether any draft amount differs from the account. Re-type any wanted figure in Manage accounts after the deploy. After it, compare `/api/Finance/balance-sheet` and `/trial-balance` totals with the figures from before.

The Documents tab has only three statuses: **Needed**, **Uploaded** and **Not needed**; every review step is gone. It lists **Still needed** (documents asked from this customer with no file yet) and **Done** (uploaded or not needed), one action set per row: Needed has **Not needed** and **Upload**, Uploaded has **View**, Not needed has **Upload**. Upload opens the file picker at once and the document is Uploaded as soon as the file arrives. A row shows "Uploading · 60%", a thin bar and a × to stop; a wrong file type or a file over 10 MB is stopped in the browser with a red message on the row. The `CustomerDocumentsThreeStatuses` migration remaps every existing requirement in one statement (Missing, Requested, Rejected, Replacement required, Postponed and Expired become Needed; Received, Under review and Approved become Uploaded; Waived and Not applicable become Not needed, with the reason, person and day copied from their latest Waived / Not applicable audit row), adds the not-needed reason, person and time, and **drops** the due date, the per-requirement and per-category file types and size, the category default due days and the file review columns (review reason, status, person and time). New calls: `POST .../customers/{id}/documents` (add a document with its first file; an existing quiet row of that type is reused, one the customer can already see is a 409), `POST .../requirements/{id}/not-needed` (reason required, only from Needed), `GET .../versions/{id}/view` (private file, no-store, nosniff, recorded as FileViewed). `POST .../upload` now replaces the current file (the old one is kept as an older file) and always leaves the document Uploaded. Removed: `POST .../requirements`, `POST .../status` and `PUT .../due-date`. The Add document popup offers every active type that is not both required and handed to every new customer (and not already on the customer). The "documents needed" number on the customer list and page is the count of required documents with status Needed.

## Booking page: Installment plan and Payments tabs (KAN-74)

The Installment plan tab is rebuilt on the shared components for desktop and phone. With no plan it shows one card, "No installment plan yet", with what is to be scheduled and a **Create plan** button; the plan form is now a popup (full screen on a phone) with Agreed sale price, Discount %, Installments, Frequency, **First due**, and an optional Possession amount and Possession due, and a strip showing what is to be scheduled and what each installment will be. **Change plan** opens the same popup filled from the current plan and replaces every installment; it shows only while the server says the plan can still be replaced. A plan shows Plan total, Paid, Remaining and Overdue (Plan total, Paid and Next due while nothing is paid), overdue due dates in red, and a **Record payment** button on every unpaid row, the possession row included. Long plans page at 20 rows on desktop; a phone shows the last two paid and the next unpaid installments with **Show all**. An orange notice with a Change plan button replaces the payment buttons whenever money is outside the plan.

The Payments tab shows Collected, Rebate credits and Outstanding and a payment history (receipt, date, what it was for, method, reference, amount) with each payment's proof or **Attach proof**, and a Receipt button that opens the receipt page inside the app.

Server changes: the date entered for the plan is now the due date of **installment 1** (it used to become one period later, so "Oct 26" was Nov 26); later installments are counted from that date, so a 31st does not drift to the 28th after February. The schedule returns its settings (installments, frequency, first due, possession amount and due date), where first due is read off the plan itself, so plans built before this change open Change plan on the right date. `unscheduledBalance` is now reported whenever what is left to pay is more than the unpaid installments hold, whatever the cause (it used to be only after a reversed rebate credit), and installment payments are refused while it is above 0 with "Change the plan for the current balance first." A plan that was built by hand or imported short of the balance is therefore also held until it is changed. Each payment row now carries `installmentSequence`, `installmentType`, `financeAccountId`, `accountName` and `recordedByName`, and the list is newest first. `POST /api/Booking/{id}/installment-plan/generate` takes an `Idempotency-Key` header like payments, so a double click never builds two plans, and building a plan is also safe against the database's own retry: a first plan whose commit was replayed reports the plan it built instead of failing with "already exists". `frequency` and `installmentStartDate` are now genuinely required on that call (they used to fall back to Monthly and year 1 when left out), and an unknown frequency is refused. Deploying this runs the `AddInstallmentPlanAttempts` migration: one new append-only table holding the unique key of each create / change plan attempt (nothing backfilled), so a replayed old attempt can never overwrite a newer plan.

**Before deploying:** because payments are now refused while money is outside the plan, a booking whose plan is short of the balance **and** already has an installment payment can be neither paid nor changed (a plan with payments cannot be replaced). Find them first and repair them by hand: a booking with installments where net price − payments − non-cash credits is more than what its unpaid installments hold, and at least one installment payment exists.

## Proof on customer payments and refunds (KAN-71)

A shared **Attach proof** upload field (one file, PDF, image, Word or Excel, up to 15 MB, checked in the browser first) is added to the component library. The server now accepts one proof file for each customer payment and each cancellation refund, using the same private storage, file checks, Admin and Accountant access and audit rows as commission and rebate proof (`POST /api/finance/commissions-rebates/evidence/CustomerPayment/{id}` and `.../CancellationRefund/{id}`). The booking detail returns `proof` (id, file name, size) on each payment and on the refund; a customer's own view of a booking never includes it.

Deploying this runs the `AddPaymentAndRefundProof` migration: two nullable columns on `FinancialEvidence`, unique filtered indexes (one file per payment or refund), and a wider "exactly one owner" check constraint. Nothing is backfilled.

## Site visit reminders (KAN-30)

Site visit reminders now follow the admin notification rule, using Pakistan calendar days. Visits without a custom reminder are announced on the configured number of days before the visit (one day by default); setting lead days to zero disables that advance reminder. A visit's own reminder time replaces the admin advance timing. The separate visit-day reminder runs only when **Remind on due date** is enabled. Turning off the Site visit reminder rule stops both reminders, while missed-visit marking and escalation continue.

Existing scheduled visits within the advance window may receive a reminder on the first scan after this release. Rescheduled visits can receive reminders again for their new schedule.
